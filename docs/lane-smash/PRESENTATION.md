# Presentation, controls and audio

## UI

Use the existing clean Rajdhani typography and dark professional menu controls. Track cards preview their palette and animated environment beneath a readable dark gradient. No visible scrollbars; the track library and history may scroll. Duration/difficulty button dimensions stay fixed. No Pause, reference-power/FTP setting or cosmetic unlock screen.

Main menu: **Settings & history → Music / Sound effects → On / Off**. Riding: **Audio → Music / Sound effects → On / Off**, then **Done** or tap outside. The timer continues while this panel is open. Both locations use the same persistent device choices. Defaults are On. Music Off does not mute effects, and Sound effects Off does not mute music. Sound effects also includes intro/recovery cues. Ghost and background motion are separate per-rider settings.

## Point counters

The HUD total and Power Burst's in-section counter sample new targets once per second, then animate at render rate. `PointRollup` uses a cubic ease-out: quick movement initially, gradual deceleration and an exact final value after **0.72 seconds**. Decreases finish in **0.30 seconds**. A newer target replaces the previous destination from the current displayed value; it never queues stale animations or overshoots. New rides and new burst sections reset their counters.

Labels and panels do not scale or bounce. Total-score digits reserve fixed width and use tabular figures. The power count is a separate fixed-width label so Pedal Faster and the points caption remain still. Metric/ghost/speed values keep their one-second snapshots. Section boundaries and safety prompts update as needed. Exact award/penalty popups and saved results are not interpolated.

These animations are presentation only. The rules, star rating, comparison key, ghost and saved score always use the exact authoritative total. The shared Effort mirror accepts both rising and falling net totals, preventing penalties from leaving a stale higher HUD score.

## Road and effects

The slightly transparent road is a fixed seeded path through space. Camera and rider move along it; the road is not reshaped toward an animated endpoint. Full motion includes curves, loops and rolls; Reduced uses gentler curves without inversions. The psychedelic background flows past at road-distance speed and shifts with resistance/cadence. Its opening follows the actual course view.

Eight palettes: Arctic, Cobalt, Copper, Titanium, Petrol, Amber, Ember, Glacier. Four environments: Prismatic flow, Kaleidoscope, Liquid aurora, Vortex. Named tracks have assigned combinations; random tracks randomize both and save them.

The smooth-suited cyclist pedals the fighter-inspired bicycle in proportion to cadence; no rear thrusters or seat highlights. Raised gold bars, mint arrows and the violet finish gate sit close to the surface and shatter on front-wheel contact. No stationary lane streak is attached beneath the rider. Cadence gradients are anchored to their section. Power floor brightness, pulses and charge build during valid output. Final-item explosions/popups mark the exact section end; adjacent sections leave target clearance.

Static bomb and incoming impact effects use red loss text, shockwave, flash and debris. Passed shots display PASSED +1,800; staying behind displays BLAST AVOIDED without a positive point award. Existing bounded pools prevent indefinite node growth.

## Sound map

| Event | Cue |
| --- | --- |
| Gold bar or ordinary gate contact | Short rounded electronic note |
| Mint arrow contact | Slightly higher, soft paired note |
| Positive ordinary section completion | Brief chord |
| Valid power accumulation | Quiet continuous harmonic bed; gain follows watts, pitch follows cadence, soft attack/release |
| Power section completion | Fuller resolving chord and soft shimmer |
| Incoming target lock | Soft warning pulse |
| Shot launch | Short rising sweep |
| Bomb/blast hit | Low thump and filtered debris noise |
| Passing a blast | Positive short chord |
| Holding back safely | Gentle whoosh, no reward fanfare |

Every logical contact/completion/shot event plays when effects are enabled. There is **no repeat cooldown, random suppression or per-second sound quota**. Sounds are pleasant through restrained gain, rounded timbres and short envelopes. A single warning/launch per shot is event identity, not throttling. Overlapping sounds use the existing eight effect voices, sufficient for the authored target density; the power bed has its own player. Muting stops active sounds immediately and does not queue them for later.

WAVs are original deterministic PCM generated by `games/tools/render_lane_smash_sfx.py`; no downloaded/licensed third-party effects are used. One-shots run 0.095–0.52 seconds with attack/tail fades. The 1-second power bed loops seamlessly. Original Night Drive remains the optional 128 BPM music. No sound is required to understand a gameplay prompt.
